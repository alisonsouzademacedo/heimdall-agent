# Screenshots

The browser subagent can take screenshots of open pages or elements on pages when it needs your review of the page state. This capability is surfaced as a tool to the agent, and you can also prompt the agent to take a screenshot of a page.

![Browser Screenshot Capture Tool](/assets/image/docs/artifacts/browser-screenshot-capture.png)

All screenshots are saved as image artifacts, and you can comment on them to give feedback to the agent.

![Browser Screenshot Artifact](/assets/image/docs/artifacts/browser-screenshot-artifact.png)
